# Cross-Pollination Strategy

Coaching, training, health timelines, competitions, and communication pipelines are now shared concepts across the entire Junior ecosystem.

## How It Works
- Core logic lives in JuniorAGI_SDK (TimelineEngine, CommunicationAgent, etc.)
- Each project consumes and specializes these concepts:
  - JuniorCoach: Primary domain for climbing/bouldering
  - JuniorHome (Swift app): Coach/athlete UI and personalization
  - JuniorSOL: On-chain training logs and competition tracking
  - JuniorDrive: Robotics training progression and sim timelines
  - JuniorStock: Performance analytics tied to training plans

This creates a consistent, intelligent experience across all apps while keeping domain focus.
